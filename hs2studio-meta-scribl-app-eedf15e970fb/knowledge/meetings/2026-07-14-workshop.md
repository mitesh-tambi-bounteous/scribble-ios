# Scribl x Bounteous Kickoff Workshop -- 2026-07-14

Four-hour kickoff workshop (Microsoft Teams, live LucidSpark board, two live
prototype demos). Bounteous facilitated the shape-phase working session: demo,
methodology walk-through, North Star, milestone back-plan, key decisions,
feature mapping, and next steps. A deeply synthesized durable retro lives in the
wiki at
[`knowledge/wiki/sources/2026-07-14-scribl-kickoff-workshop.md`](../wiki/sources/2026-07-14-scribl-kickoff-workshop.md);
this digest is the operational record and action-item seam.

## TL;DR

- The room committed to a path into Phase 0 and reset the timeline: the PRFAQ's
  Aug 1 / Sep 15 dates are off the table ("very fictitious," Matt Kaplan). Everything
  now anchors on the AWS re:Invent keynote (~2026-12-05) and is product-readiness
  driven; beta / public-launch / first-submission dates are TBD pending Scribl's
  internal sync.
- All eight key feature decisions were made and mostly matched Bounteous's
  pre-read recommendations.
- The working prototype earned strong buy-in ("I would be out pitching this
  tomorrow," Matt); both founders closed with clear commitment ("We've been
  waiting 20 years for this. Let's go," Jeff Sparr).
- Claude ambition rose above the conservative baseline: the room wants Claude
  visibly central from beta, as both a user differentiator and an Anthropic-funding
  magnet.
- Comments were removed from the product live during the second demo; the room's
  aversion to social-media negativity became a product principle.

## Decisions

Owner is the person who made or ratified the call.

- Voice memo / dictation response: fast-follow after MLP; typed story text stays
  in for both platforms (Matt Kaplan). Rationale: typing suffices for MLP speed;
  Android audio storage is heavy.
- Paywall timing: post-launch (Matt Kaplan). Beta gathers maximum data; run-cost
  and artifact-cost are unknown, so pricing waits.
- Personalized follow-ups: real personalization is post-MLP; scaffold from the
  start and use generic + event-based pushes ("Matt just drew on your wall") in
  the interim (Eric Rice, Rob Forshier II, Martin Young).
- Age gate / minors (COPPA): ship 13+ first for beta and public launch; pursue
  under-13 as a separate submission after launch, with in-app "coming soon"
  messaging for minors (Martin Young). Apple minor-protection + COPPA add a
  month-plus and heavy reporting, threatening the re:Invent window.
- Moderation fail policy: fail-safe -- hold submissions if moderation is down;
  richer degradation UX post-MLP (Matt Kaplan, to Bounteous's lead). "If we're
  gonna fail, let's be safe."
- Claude integration depth: Claude is a beta-visible differentiator (personalized
  and context-aware prompts, drawing reflection, empathy-oriented agent); the
  Exciting Artifact stays a seam (David Lawton, Martin Young).
- Partner API "at launch" claim: post-MLP; Scribl amends the PRFAQ (Matt Kaplan).
- Channel model: a fixed set of predefined, renamable channels for MLP, tagged by
  wall type (family / friends / coworkers) for segmentation; unlimited channels
  behind the paywall (David Lawton, Matt Kaplan). Exact number left open (instinct
  converged near three to four).
- Claude hosting: confirmed on Amazon Bedrock, settled outside the workshop and
  tied to the AWS funding arrangement; the provider abstraction stays swappable.
- Comments: removed from the product (room consensus, Helen Leffers / Eric Rice /
  Martin Young). Reactions-as-drawings floated as a future idea.

## Blockers

- Cost uncertainty (run-cost, artifact-cost) blocks the pricing / paywall model
  (Matt Kaplan).
- Scribl cannot easily parse its own usage data today (Metabase); accessible
  reporting for staff is a hard requirement for launch (Eric Rice, Abigail Knapton).

- Whether Apple's parental-approval ("ask-to-buy") flow mitigates the under-13

- All launch dates, pending Scribl's internal sync.
- Bounteous / Mission Cloud vendor boundary on the AI-enhancement pipeline.
- AI-generated daily prompts (PRFAQ) vs a curated preset list.
